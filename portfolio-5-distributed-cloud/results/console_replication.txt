### A4. Synchronous standby outage: consistency over availability
With pg-replica-sync stopped, a COMMIT on the primary is still waiting after 4s; pg_stat_activity shows wait_event = `IPC/SyncRep`.
After restarting the standby the COMMIT returned (total 6.2s). No acknowledged write can exist on only one node. The escape hatch for availability is `SET synchronous_commit = local` (or `ANY 1 (replica_sync, replica_async)` quorum), accepting possible loss on failover.
